Privacy Policy — apps and games by KPB

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy covers the apps and games published on the App Store by Kevin Bland ("KPB", "we"): the games Catacomb Snake, The Great Webscape, Poo Bat!, Catacombs and Dragon Canyon, and the app MyCollage. We're a one-person developer based in the United Kingdom. We've kept them as close to "nothing leaves your phone" as we can. This page explains the few exceptions.

What stays on your phone

Your scores, initials, progress, collages and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

We don't use accounts, analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell or share your information.

Your photos (MyCollage)

MyCollage uses only the photos you choose to put in a collage, and saves the finished collage to your photo library when you ask it to. Your photos and collages are never uploaded or sent to us or anyone else, unless you share a collage yourself.

If you send us feedback

The Feedback screen in each app and game lets you send us a message. When you choose to send one, these go to a Google Form we own:

  • your message;
  • your email address, only if you choose to give it so we can reply;
  • the app's name, and technical details that help us fix problems: its version, your device model and iOS version, and in a game how far you've got and your initials.

We use this only to read your feedback and, if you asked, to reply. The form is run by Google LLC on our behalf (see Google's privacy policy). To have a message deleted, send us a note through the same Feedback screen.

Game Center (some games)

Some games use Apple's Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. That uses your Game Center account and is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We see only what Game Center shows every player, such as the scores on a leaderboard.

Advertising (some apps and games)

Some apps and games show ads, provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device. This includes device details, your approximate location worked out from your IP address, and how you interact with the ads.

  • If you're in the UK or Europe, you're asked for your consent first, and no ads are requested until you've answered. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy choices in the settings, where shown.
  • We don't ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps (Apple's "App Tracking Transparency"). So Google can't use your device's advertising identifier, and the ads you see are less personalised.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

How Google uses this information is explained at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

These apps and games aren't designed for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us feedback with personal details, tell us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how it's used. Contact us through the Feedback screen in any of them, or at: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd6ZWBBHSrNyhGJgRUkxN9PerUdEuDK3b0h_965D7oq-idS4A/viewform.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
